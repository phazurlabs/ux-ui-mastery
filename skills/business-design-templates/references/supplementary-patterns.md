### Red Flags in Client Contracts

- "Work made for hire" language (may void your portfolio rights)
- IP transfer before final payment
- Non-compete clauses (you cannot work for competitors)
- Unlimited revisions
- Perpetual NDA with no portfolio exception
- Liability exceeding project fee
- Vague scope with fixed price (scope creep trap)
- Payment on "acceptance" with no defined acceptance criteria
